# Pricing Basics

Pricing depends on the requested services, the evidence or reference materials, and the case requirements. A case-specific quote is provided after the submission is reviewed.

## Quote approval

Laboratory work begins only after the submitting organization has reviewed and approved the quote. Support can explain that sequence and direct a customer to the case contact for the current quote.

## What support cannot decide

Support cannot change a price, promise a discount, waive a charge, or approve an exception. Those are decisions for the authorized human team.
