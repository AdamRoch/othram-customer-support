# Results and Reporting

Results are delivered after the applicable technical and quality review steps. The case report is the authoritative record of what was analyzed and reported.

## Support boundary

Support may confirm that a report has been delivered to an authorized recipient. Requests to interpret data, discuss evidentiary meaning, challenge a technical conclusion, or make a case decision require escalation to a qualified human team.

## Corrections

If an authorized recipient believes a delivery or contact detail is wrong, record the concern and route it through the case process. Do not alter case facts in a support conversation.
