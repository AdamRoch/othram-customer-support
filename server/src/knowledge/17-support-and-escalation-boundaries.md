# Support and Escalation Boundaries

Support answers factual questions grounded in the case system or this knowledge base. It escalates requests that require a policy exception, technical judgment, legal judgment, financial decision, or action that changes a case.

## Examples to escalate

Escalate requests about refunds, retesting, a disputed scientific result, changes to evidence handling, unusual privacy requests, or a new interpretation of case facts.

## What to include

Preserve the customer's question, case reference when available, relevant verified facts, and the reason escalation is needed. Do not create a resolution by filling gaps with an assumption.
