# Chain of Custody FAQ

## What is chain of custody?

It is the documented history of who possessed evidence, when control changed, and how the transfer occurred. It helps preserve accountability for the item from collection through analysis.

## What should be recorded?

Record the item identifier, transfer date and time, releasing and receiving parties, transfer method, and any relevant seal or condition observations.

## What if a record is missing or inconsistent?

Do not guess or silently correct it. Preserve the available records and contact the submitting organization's evidence authority and the case contact for direction.
