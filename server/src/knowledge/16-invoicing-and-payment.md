# Invoicing and Payment

Billing communications should use the case reference and the approved quote or invoice. Keep payment information in the approved payment process; do not place account or payment details in an ordinary support message.

## Common questions

Support can help locate the appropriate billing contact or confirm that a billing question has been routed. Requests for refunds, charge changes, alternative terms, or disputed amounts require human review.

## Case impact

If a billing issue may affect a case, do not speculate about laboratory timing. Ask the authorized case or billing team to provide the next verified update.
