# Evidence Packaging and Shipping Requirements

Follow the case-specific instructions supplied by the laboratory and the evidence policies of the submitting organization. Those instructions control if they differ from this overview.

## Packaging basics

Keep items identified, sealed, and separated. Use packaging appropriate to the evidence type and avoid conditions that could introduce contamination, moisture, loss, or damage. Include the approved documentation and case reference where instructed.

## Shipping basics

Do not mail evidence to a generic address or use an unapproved shipping method. Use the assigned destination and trackable carrier method. Keep shipment records with the case file until receipt is confirmed.
