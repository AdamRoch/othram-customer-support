# Kinship Testing Process

## Define the question

The submitting authority identifies the proposed relationship and the reference materials that may be relevant. The technical team evaluates whether the request can be addressed with the available evidence.

## Analyze and review

Laboratory and analytical work is reviewed before any report is delivered. A relationship assessment is expressed in the context of the stated hypothesis and the available data.

## Communicate results

The authorized recipient receives the result through the case process. Support can confirm status but does not interpret a relationship result or advise on legal consequences.
