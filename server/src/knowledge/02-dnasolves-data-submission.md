# DNASolves DNA Data Submission

Participants can submit eligible DNA data through their DNASolves account. Follow the account instructions for the accepted file format and the current upload process.

## Before submitting

Confirm that the account belongs to you and that you are authorized to provide the data. Review the consent language in the submission flow carefully; it explains the program purpose and choices available at the time of submission.

## If an upload fails

Do not email raw DNA files to support. Keep the error message, verify the account and file selected, and contact DNASolves support through the secure account channel for help.
