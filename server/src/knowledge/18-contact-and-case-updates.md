# Contact and Case Updates

Use the authorized case channel for substantive updates. Include the case reference and a concise explanation of the request so the receiving team can find the correct record.

## Updating contact information

Requests to change contact details require the applicable identity and authority check. Support should record the request and route it rather than changing protected case information based only on an inbound message.

## Urgent concerns

For a shipment problem, possible evidence issue, or time-sensitive legal matter, preserve the details and alert the authorized case contact promptly. Support should not give legal advice or make an operational exception on its own.
