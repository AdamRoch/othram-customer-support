# DNASolves Privacy and Consent

DNASolves participation is voluntary. Before providing DNA data, participants should read the applicable privacy notice and consent language in the service they are using.

## What to consider

Genetic information can have implications for biological relatives. Submit only information you are entitled to provide, and do not make assumptions about another person's relationship to a case from a database result.

## Questions and choices

Privacy requests and account questions should be sent through the designated DNASolves support or privacy channel. This knowledge base summarizes the process; the current published notice and submitted consent control.
