# Forensic DNA Process Overview

Forensic DNA work moves through controlled stages: evidence receipt, extraction, quantification, library preparation, sequencing, bioinformatics, review, and delivery. A case can also be marked delayed without changing its underlying stage.

## Why stages matter

Each stage has its own technical purpose and review needs. The case system is the authority for a case's current stage and any expected timeline; support should report those facts rather than inventing dates.

## Results

Not every item yields the same amount or quality of usable data. Scientific questions about method selection or interpretation belong with the technical team.
