# Privacy and Data Handling FAQ

## What information may be involved?

Case submissions can include contact, case, DNA, genetic, and genealogical information. Handle each according to applicable law, the submitter's authority, and the current published privacy notice.

## Can support disclose case details?

Support verifies identity before discussing case status and does not disclose protected details merely because a caller knows a case number. Requests beyond ordinary status updates are routed to the authorized case contact.

## Where is the controlling policy?

The current published privacy notice and any case-specific terms control. This FAQ is an operational summary, not a replacement for those documents.
